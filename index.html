Why group gifts?

One shared gift can mean more than a pile of small ones. It is less to buy, less to wrap, and less to find room for. It's kinder to your wallet and to the planet, too.

About

I am Catherine Ilic, and I live in London. Picture My Gift grew out of my master's research at the London Interdisciplinary School, where I looked at why a shared gift is so easy to welcome and so hard to suggest.

Both the Christmas page and the birthday kit are free to try, and both are new. The Christmas page was made for this season. It comes from the birthday kit, which is where the idea started.

Questions or ideas? Write to hello@picturemygift.com, or find me on LinkedIn. You can also follow Picture My Gift on Instagram (@picture.my.gift).

Privacy. This website saves nothing about you and uses no analytics or advertising. The Christmas page keeps everything inside the link you share, so nothing is stored. The birthday kit saves the party details and replies for as long as the party needs, and deletes them 28 days after the party date. We never handle payments: people pay the organiser's own payment link.
