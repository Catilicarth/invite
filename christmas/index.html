About

Would a group gift make Christmas simpler?
Often, yes. A class can give a teacher one present instead of a pile of small ones. Relatives can give one shared gift to someone in the family. Colleagues can do the same for someone in the team.
Made for people who want to set up a group gift quickly. You say who the gift is for and what it is, and you get a message ready to paste into your group chat: WhatsApp, Signal or whatever your group uses. Anyone who wants to join pays you directly through your own payment link. The page never handles money, and nothing is saved.
It's free and super easy to use. It comes from the birthday kit, which helps parents plan collective birthday gifts, and which grew out of my master's research at the London Interdisciplinary School.

How it works

Made for setting up a group gift quickly, through the group chat you already use.
1
Choose who the gift is for. Someone at school, a family member, a colleague, or anyone else.
2
Add the gift idea and your payment link.
3
Check your payment link. Once the link is sent, it cannot be changed.
4
Send the ready-made message to your group chat (WhatsApp, Signal or whatever you use). The message is a template. Change it however you like before you send it, so it sounds like you.
People in the group tap the link if they want to contribute.

Privacy

Everything is in the link. We store nothing: no names, no photos, no payment details, no cookies, no analytics.
The link is as private as the chat you share it in.
Questions or feedback? Write to hello@picturemygift.com.

FAQ

Does the money go through this app?
No. People in the group pay your own payment link directly.
What if someone prefers not to join?
They don't reply or pay. Nothing is tracked.
Who sees what people give?
Other people in the group can't. You may see payments in your own payment account.
What if more is raised than needed?
It is up to you. You can put it towards something extra for the gift or pass it on to a charity. This app can't do it for you.
Is there a target amount?
Optional. It is information only, because the app can't see what has been paid.
